# Scouting-Ashanti
Scouting Ashanti is dedicated to promoting the spirit of Scouting throughout the Ashanti Region of Ghana by empowering young people with the values, skills, and leadership qualities needed to become responsible citizens and future leaders
Through outdoor adventures, camping, community service, teamwork, discipline, and leadership development, Scouting Ashanti provides opportunities for young people to discover their potential, build confidence, develop practical life skills, and contribute positively to society.
Guided by the Scout Promise and Law, Scouting Ashanti encourages respect, honesty, patriotism, service to others, environmental responsibility, and peaceful coexistence. It also provides a platform for Scouts and Scout leaders to connect, share experiences, and work together towards the growth and development of Scouting across the region.
Our Vision: To develop responsible, disciplined, and service-oriented young people who will make a positive impact in their communities and the nation.
Our Mission: To contribute to the growth of Scouting in the Ashanti Region through youth empowerment, leadership training, community engagement, outdoor education, and the promotion of the values of Scouting.
Our Motto: Be Prepared!
